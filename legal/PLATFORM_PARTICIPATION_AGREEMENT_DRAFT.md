## 1. Platform services
The Platform provides software tools for load listings, freight requests, carrier bids, dispatch administration, shipment documentation, and communications. The Platform does not undertake to transport freight or act as a freight broker merely by providing software. Actual activities and separate agreements determine any regulated status.

## 2. Independent operations and authority
Participant operates its own independent business and is responsible for maintaining all licenses, registrations, operating authority, insurance, and qualified personnel required for its activities. Participant shall not represent itself as an employee or agent of the Platform absent a separately signed written authorization.

## 3. Shipment responsibility
The party actually contracting to perform transportation remains responsible for its own transportation services, including driver conduct, vehicle maintenance, cargo handling, securement, accidents, and legally applicable claims. This provision does not transfer responsibility for the Platform's own conduct or override mandatory law.

## 4. Insurance verification
Before being eligible for load awards, each participating carrier must submit current certificates of insurance meeting applicable legal and Platform requirements, with the correct Platform legal entity listed as certificate holder where required. The Platform may independently verify coverage and suspend access if verification fails or coverage lapses. Certificate-holder status alone does not grant additional-insured rights.

## 5. Customer and driver communications
Participant authorizes the Platform to store communications made within the Platform and contact-log entries voluntarily submitted by authorized users for shipment administration, dispute resolution, and compliance purposes. Communications made through personal devices or external services will not be automatically captured without a supported integration and any required permissions. Audio recording requires separately obtained consent when legally required. Participant must give required privacy notices to its users and customers.

## 6. Load bids and acceptance
A submitted bid is an offer on the displayed shipment terms. A load award is binding only upon acceptance under the Platform's specified booking workflow and any applicable rate confirmation or transportation agreement. The Platform may maintain timestamped audit records of bids, acceptances, and amendments.

## 7. Liability and indemnification
To the fullest extent permitted by applicable law, Participant is responsible for third-party claims arising from its own negligent or wrongful acts, omissions, regulatory violations, or breach of this Agreement, and shall indemnify the Platform against such claims, except to the extent caused by the Platform's negligence, willful misconduct, or other liability that cannot legally be excluded. Any limitations of liability must be separately and conspicuously stated in final counsel-approved terms.

## 8. Electronic records and signatures
The parties agree to conduct this transaction electronically and consent to electronic records and signatures. Each signer represents that they are authorized to bind the entity they identify. The Platform will present the complete agreement before signature, permit review, retain the executed version and an audit trail, and provide each signer a downloadable copy. Any consumer electronic-record disclosures and consent procedures required by law must be completed separately.
