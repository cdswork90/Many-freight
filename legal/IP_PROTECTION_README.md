## Immediate security steps
- Keep the source repository private where appropriate and restrict collaborator permissions.
- Enable MFA on GitHub, Vercel, Supabase, domain registrar, and email.
- Remove/rotate any exposed credentials and avoid committing .env or secrets.
- Keep dated commits, invoices, contractor agreements, design files, and evidence of original authorship.
- Consider US Copyright Office registration for eligible human-authored software and USPTO trademark clearance/registration for the brand.
- Have a qualified attorney review the ownership chain and signed agreements.

**Note:** Existing public repository visibility may already expose source or files. A notice alone does not revoke rights granted under an existing license.
