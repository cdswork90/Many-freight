## Confidential Information
Nonpublic code, architecture, credentials, pricing, customer data, business plans, product designs, and other information identified as confidential or reasonably understood to be confidential. Each party will use Confidential Information only for the Purpose, limit access to personnel with a need to know who are bound by protective duties, apply reasonable security, and not disclose it except as permitted in writing or required by law (with legally permitted notice).

## Ownership and no license
Disclosure transfers no ownership or license in intellectual property except the limited right to evaluate the Purpose. No reverse engineering of disclosed nonpublic software except where prohibited from restriction by law.
